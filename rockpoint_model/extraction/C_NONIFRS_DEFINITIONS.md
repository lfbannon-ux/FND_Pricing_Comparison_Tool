# Agent C — Non-IFRS and Supplementary Measures, Rockpoint Gas Storage Inc.

**Source:** Quartr documentId `4064736` — *Management's Discussion and Analysis For the Fiscal Year Ended
March 31, 2026*, MD&A dated **May 27, 2026** (filed 2026-05-28). All 39 pages read.
Page deep-links are of the form
`https://web.quartr.com/companies/21558?companyId=21558&documentId=4064736&documentType=report&eventId=554427&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&rp=<page>`.

**Basis:** Every non-IFRS measure in this MD&A is presented on the **`business_100`** basis — the Business
(Swan OpCo + BIF OpCo and subsidiaries) on a 100% basis. The section is literally titled *"Non-IFRS Measures
Utilized by **Our Business**"* (p.15). **No non-IFRS measure is presented on the `company` basis anywhere in
this MD&A.** In particular there is **no Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Gross Margin, Distributable Cash Flow or
Net Debt for Rockpoint Gas Storage Inc. itself**, and **no Distributable Cash Flow per share** on either basis.

## 0. Blanket caution printed by the issuer

> "We report our financial results in accordance with IFRS. However, management believes that certain non-IFRS
> financial measures provide investors with useful information in evaluating our performance. Management believes
> that excluding certain items that may vary substantially in frequency and magnitude period-to-period from net
> earnings provides useful supplemental measures that assist in evaluating our ability to generate earnings and
> cash flow, and more readily compare these metrics between past and future periods. These non-IFRS financial
> measures are not standardized measures under IFRS and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used
> by other companies." — p.15

> "Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Gross Margin, Distributable Cash Flow and Net Debt are non-IFRS financial measures
> and ratios and are used by our management and by external users of our financial statements, such as investors,
> research analysts and others, to assess the financial performance of our assets over the long-term to generate
> sufficient cash to service indebtedness, fund maintenance and growth capital projects and to make distributions
> to our shareholders." — p.15

## 1. Adjusted EBITDA — non-IFRS financial measure

**Verbatim definition (p.16):**

> "We define Adjusted EBITDA, which we use as the primary non-IFRS financial measure of profitability to evaluate
> the performance of our Business, as net earnings adjusted by financing costs, income tax (benefit) expense,
> depreciation and amortization, unrealized risk management losses (gains), and other (income) expenses."

**Verbatim rationale (p.16):**

> "We believe that Adjusted EBITDA is meaningful because it presents the financial performance of our Business on
> a basis which excludes the impact of certain non-cash items, items whose impact is external to ordinary course
> operations, extraordinary items, as well as how the operations have been financed."

- **Nearest IFRS measure:** Net earnings.
- **Reconciliation:** p.17 (four columns) and re-presented as a build-up on p.20.
- **Periods presented:** Q4 FY2026, Q4 FY2025, FY2026, FY2025 (p.17, p.20); all eight quarters
  Q1 FY2025 – Q4 FY2026 (p.21).
- **Printed values (USD millions):** Q4 FY2026 109.2 · Q4 FY2025 112.7 · FY2026 385.9 · FY2025 338.8.
- **Definition change:** none disclosed.

## 2. Adjusted EBITDA Margin — non-IFRS ratio

**Verbatim definition (p.16):**

> "Adjusted EBITDA when expressed as a percentage of Adjusted Gross Margin, as defined below, ('Adjusted EBITDA
> Margin') is a non-IFRS ratio calculated as Adjusted EBITDA divided by Adjusted Gross Margin. Adjusted EBITDA
> Margin is used by our management and by external investors to assess efficiency in managing operating and
> general and administrative expenses relative to our Adjusted Gross Margin."

- **Note the unusual denominator:** the denominator is **Adjusted Gross Margin, not revenue**. A reader who
  assumes "EBITDA margin = EBITDA / revenue" will be wrong (FY2026: 385.9 / 459.1 = 84%, whereas
  385.9 / 479.4 revenue = 80%).
- **Periods presented:** Q4 FY2026 85% · Q4 FY2025 84% · FY2026 84% · FY2025 82% (p.20).
- **Definition change:** none disclosed.

## 3. Adjusted Gross Margin — non-IFRS financial measure

**Verbatim definition (p.16):**

> "We define Adjusted Gross Margin, which we use as a non-IFRS financial measure of profitability, as net earnings
> adjusted by financing costs, income tax (benefit) expense, depreciation and amortization, unrealized risk
> management losses (gains), other (income) expenses, operating, general and administrative expenses and other
> items."

**Verbatim rationale (p.16):**

> "We believe that Adjusted Gross Margin is a useful measure of profitability because it presents our residual
> earnings after deducting the direct costs of gas storage services from our Fee-for-Service and realized
> optimization revenue."

- **Nearest IFRS measure:** Net earnings.
- **Reconciliation:** p.17 (from Adjusted EBITDA, adding back Operating, General and administrative, Other items);
  build-up from revenue components on p.20.
- **Periods presented:** Q4 FY2026, Q4 FY2025, FY2026, FY2025 (p.17, p.20); all eight quarters (p.21).
- **Printed values (USD millions):** Q4 FY2026 128.7 · Q4 FY2025 134.2 · FY2026 459.1 · FY2025 412.4.
- **Definition change:** none disclosed.

## 4. Fee-for-Service gross margin as a percentage of Adjusted Gross Margin — non-IFRS ratio

**Verbatim definition (p.16):**

> "Fee-for-Service gross margin, which is net of cost of gas storage services, as a percentage of Adjusted Gross
> Margin is a non-IFRS ratio and is calculated as Fee-for-Service gross margin divided by Adjusted Gross Margin.
> Fee-for-Service gross margin as a percentage of Adjusted Gross Margin is used by our management and by external
> investors to determine the proportion of Adjusted Gross Margin that is driven by Fee-for-Service gross margin."

- The p.20 table caption is printed as **"Fee-for-Service as a %of Adjusted Gross Margin"** (missing space in
  "%of"); transcribed verbatim in the CSV.
- **Periods presented:** Q4 FY2026 77% · Q4 FY2025 78% · FY2026 83% · FY2025 86% (p.20).
- **Component measure — "Fee-for-Service gross margin"** is built on p.20 as
  `ToP` + `STS, net of cost of gas storage services` = `Total Fee-for-Service gross margin`
  (FY2026: 233.6 + 146.7 = 380.3; FY2025: 185.0 + 170.8 = 355.8).
  Note the asymmetry: the **ToP** component is gross revenue as reported, while **only the STS** component is
  reduced by cost of gas storage services (all of the reported cost of gas storage services, 8.2 in FY2026 and
  11.0 in FY2025, is charged against STS).
- **Definition change:** none disclosed.

## 5. Distributable Cash Flow — non-IFRS financial measure

**Verbatim definition (p.16):**

> "In addition to Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Gross Margin, we utilize Distributable Cash Flow as a non-IFRS
> financial measure of profitability to provide insights into the cash earnings that are available for
> distribution, to buy back shares, fund working capital requirements, and/or reinvest in our Business to further
> enhance growth. We define Distributable Cash Flow as net earnings adjusted by financing costs, income tax
> (benefit) expense, depreciation and amortization, unrealized risk management losses (gains), other (income)
> expenses, interest expense, mandatory debt repayments, current taxes, cash lease payments, maintenance capital
> expenditures and other items."

**Verbatim rationale (p.16):**

> "We believe that Distributable Cash Flow is a meaningful financial metric because it presents our cash earnings
> that are available for distribution, to buy back shares, and/or reinvest in our Business."

- **Nearest IFRS measure:** Net earnings (the p.17 table reconciles to net earnings, **not** to cash from
  operating activities).
- **Reconciliation:** p.17.
- **Periods presented:** Q4 FY2026, Q4 FY2025, FY2026, FY2025 (p.17, p.20); all eight quarters (p.21).
- **Printed values (USD millions):** Q4 FY2026 74.8 · Q4 FY2025 78.0 · FY2026 251.6 · FY2025 234.5.
- **DEFINITION / MEASUREMENT ADJUSTMENTS disclosed in the p.17 footnotes — see §8 below.**
- **Distributable Cash Flow *per share* is NOT presented anywhere in this MD&A.**

## 7. Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA — non-IFRS ratio

**Verbatim definition (p.16):**

> "Net debt when expressed as a multiple of Adjusted EBITDA is a non-IFRS ratio calculated by dividing net debt by

## 8. Footnotes to the p.17 reconciliation — verbatim (these ARE the definition qualifiers)

1. > "Excludes the net unrealized impact from interest rate swaps, which are included within financing costs."
   — attaches to *Unrealized risk management losses (gains) (1)*. Verified: p.30 net unrealized total less the
   interest-rate-swap component reproduces the printed add-back in all four columns.

2. > "Other items consists of net unrealized electricity contract gains/losses."
   — attaches to both *Other items (2)* rows (the add-back into Adjusted Gross Margin and the deduction into
   Distributable Cash Flow, which are equal and opposite in every column).

3. > "Interest expense includes interest on non-affiliated debt obligations, including the Term Loan due 2026,
   > Term Loan due 2031, the ABL Facility, the Warwick Credit Facility and other interest income (expense), net of
   > realized gains on interest rate swaps."

4. > "Excludes a one-time payment of $19.3 million made during the three months ended September 30, 2025 related
   > to modified storage leases. All future payments for the related leases were eliminated in exchange for one
   > upfront payment. Excludes variable lease payments included in operating."
   — **MEASUREMENT EXCLUSION affecting FY2026 Distributable Cash Flow.** Printed FY2026 cash lease payments in the
   DCF reconciliation are $8.6 million; a further $19.3 million of cash was actually paid in Q2 FY2026 and is
   excluded. Had it been included, FY2026 DCF would have been $19.3 million lower. **This exclusion is
   FY2026-specific and has no FY2025 counterpart, so the FY2026 vs FY2025 DCF comparison (+$17.1 million, +7%)
   is not like-for-like on a cash basis.**

5. > "Fiscal 2025 maintenance capital expenditures were adjusted downwards to reflect $5.5 million in one-time
   > costs associated with historical heat imbalances and cushion gas migration."
   — **DEFINITION ADJUSTMENT to the FY2025 comparative.** The p.26 capital-expenditure table prints FY2025
   maintenance capital expenditures of **$26.8 million**; the DCF reconciliation uses **$21.3 million**
   (26.8 − 5.5 = 21.3, confirmed). FY2026 has no such adjustment (26.4 in both tables). **FY2025 Distributable
   Cash Flow is therefore $5.5 million higher than it would be on the FY2026 measurement convention.**

## 9. Supplementary financial measures (issuer's own label — p.18)

### Contracted Fee-for-Service revenue backlog
**Verbatim definition (p.18):**

> "Contracted Fee-for-Service revenue backlog is a supplementary financial measure that represents the cumulative
> contracted revenues for our ToP and STS services, not yet recognized as revenue in the Financial Statements of
> the Business. Contracted Fee-for-Service revenue backlog is an operating measure that we use to evaluate the
> effectiveness of our contracting strategies and the stability of cash flow generation in the coming years."

**Verbatim footnote (printed under both the California and the Alberta roll-forward, p.18):**

> "Contracted Fee-for-Service revenue backlog excludes fuel and commodity recovery associated with ToP contracts."

- **Periods presented:** FY2026 and FY2025 roll-forwards, separately for **California** and **Alberta**.
- **Ending balances:** California $784.4m (2026-03-31) / $737.7m (2025-03-31); Alberta $162.7m / $155.5m.
  **No combined portfolio total is printed** (the sum, $947.1m, is not a disclosed figure).
- Only the Alberta roll-forward carries a *"Foreign currency adjustments and other"* line (Alberta contracts are
  CAD-denominated).
- **Definition change:** none disclosed.

### Other operating measures defined in narrative (pp.3, 19)

- **Effective working gas storage capacity (p.3):**
  > "We define 'effective working gas storage capacity' as the maximum volume of natural gas that can be stored in
  > an underground storage facility in accordance with its design less base gas, where base gas (or cushion gas)
  > is the volume of natural gas intended as permanent inventory in a storage reservoir to maintain adequate
  > pressure and deliverability rates throughout the withdrawal season. Management conducts its own estimates for
  > total effective working gas storage capacity and routinely commissions third-party reservoir analyses of its
  > reservoirs to validate the reservoir operating parameter."
- **ToP contracted storage rates (p.19):**
  > "ToP contracted storage rates refers to the weighted average demand charge paid by our long-term firm storage
  > service contract customers."
- **Short-term Storage contract rates (p.19):**
  > "Under STS contract arrangements, which typically span up to one storage season, our customers pay a fixed fee
  > to inject and withdraw specified quantities of natural gas."

**Rate-basis trap on p.19/p.9:** the p.19 table column *"Contract Rate ($/Dth (1))"* is stated **including** fuel
and commodity recovery (FY2026: California 2.93, Alberta 1.00), while footnote 1 gives the same rates **excluding**
fuel and commodity recovery (FY2026: California 2.87, Alberta 0.95; FY2025: 2.30 and 0.75). The p.9 narrative
figure of **"approximately $2.32 per Dth"** (FY2025: $1.86) is the capacity-weighted average on the **excluding**
basis (recomputed: 2.313 and 1.861). The capacity-weighted average on the **including** basis is 2.370 / 1.926 and
is **not** printed. Do not mix the two.
