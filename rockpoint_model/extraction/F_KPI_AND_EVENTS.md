## 7. Company-basis reporting period — the acquisition boundary

Rockpoint Gas Storage Inc. was **incorporated 2025-07-28** and **acquired its 40% interest in the
Business on 2025-10-15** (IPO closing). It **held no interest in the Business before that date**.
Company-basis figures therefore cover:

| Release | Company-basis periods printed | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 FY2026 | 3M ended 2025-12-31 **and** 2025-07-28 → 2025-12-31; balance sheet at 2025-12-31 vs **2025-07-28 (date of incorporation)** | 7–9 |
| **FY2026** | 3M ended 2026-03-31 **and** 2025-07-28 → 2026-03-31; balance sheet at 2026-03-31 vs **2025-07-28** | **8–10** |
| Q1 FY2027 | 3M ended 2026-06-30 only (single column); balance sheet at 2026-06-30 vs 2026-03-31 | 8–10 |

Every comparative column dated **2025-07-28 is entirely zero** (printed as `$ -`). The FY2026 release
footnotes it: *"Comparative period is the date of incorporation."* The Q4 FY2026 release adds:
*"These statements are presented on a **standalone** basis."*

**Consequence for the Q3 FY2026 income statement:** the three-month column and the
inception-to-date column print **identical amounts for every line except EPS** (net earnings $25.1m
in both). The Company had no operations before 2025-10-15, so the quarter contains the entire
post-acquisition period. EPS differs ($0.56 vs $0.95) purely because the weighted-average share
counts differ. This is not an error and is flagged row-by-row in the CSV.

**No non-IFRS measure is ever presented on the Company (40%) basis.** Adjusted Gross Margin,
Adjusted EBITDA, Distributable Cash Flow, DCF per share, Fee-for-Service % of AGM and Net Debt are
all **100%-basis (`business_100`) only**, in every release.
